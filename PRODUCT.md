# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Russian-speaking adults (18–45) who want to watch something tonight. They're on the couch, usually on mobile, often with a partner nearby. They've already opened Netflix/Кинопоиск and felt overwhelmed. They want someone to just tell them what to watch — not another catalog to browse.

## Product Purpose

Kinopolka eliminates decision paralysis around choosing a film or series. A 3-step quiz, a one-click roulette, or a shared partner session replaces 30–60 minutes of aimless scrolling. Success = clicking "Watch" or adding to Watchlist within 30 seconds (TTW < 30s).

## Brand Personality

Decisive · Cinematic · Confident

## Anti-references

- **Netflix**: Too commercial, mainstream red, catalog-browser feel
- **Кинопоиск/КиноАфиша**: Dense portal, overwhelming, too many links
- **Generic dark SaaS**: Gray-on-gray with no identity (our former state)
- **Letterboxd**: Too critic/community-focused, too minimal for decision-making
- **Cream/warm AI default**: The 2026 beige trap — warm-tinted neutrals with no brand character

## Design Principles

1. **Guide, don't present**: Every screen moves the user toward a decision, not gives them more to scroll
2. **Cinema as stage**: Interface is the dark room; posters are the light source
3. **Hierarchy earns attention**: One primary action per screen; secondary actions recede visually
4. **Speed is personality**: Transitions are fast and decisive, no lingering ornamental animation
5. **Posters carry the emotion**: Typography and chrome step back when media is present

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA minimum; all body text ≥ 4.5:1 contrast on dark backgrounds
- All transitions respect `prefers-reduced-motion`
- Focus states visible via crimson ring in all contexts
- Touch targets ≥ 44px on all interactive elements
